# Data source and dictionary

The original case survey is in [microvan.csv](microvan.csv). It contains 400 screened survey respondents, sampled for an academic case from consumers with nonzero near-term purchase interest. This is not an unrestricted representative sample of the U.S. market.

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| subjnumb | Anonymous respondent identifier |
| mvliking | Overall concept liking, 1–9 |
| kidtrans through accesfun | 30 attitudinal survey responses, each 1–9 |
| age | Age in years |
| income | Annual household income in thousands of USD |
| miles | Annual household mileage in thousands |
| numkids | Children aged 0–18 in household |
| female | Binary indicator as recorded in source |
| educ | 1 high school, 2 some college, 3 undergraduate, 4 graduate |
| recycle | Self-reported recycling relative to average, 1–5 |

Source: *Grosse Pointe Associates and the “Microvan”*, capstone case, Modern Marketing Research: Concepts, Methods, and Cases. Case data are distinct from simulated intervention and scenario cost records. See [research contributions and data provenance](SIMULATION_PROVENANCE.md). No realized sales, campaign outcomes or causal effects were collected in the survey.
